04 / Priority 1 — Start here

Build the right foundation
before chasing traffic.

A practical launch checklist for a website that people can use, search engines can understand and you can measure.

Before launch

Website essentials

  • Mobile-friendly layout and accessible navigation
  • Fast page loading and image optimisation
  • HTTPS, working forms and clear contact details
  • Unique page titles, meta descriptions and headings
  • Useful original copy for each service and location
  • Clean URLs, internal links and a custom 404 page
After launch

Google visibility setup

  • Verify the domain in Google Search Console
  • Publish and submit an XML sitemap
  • Check indexing and fix crawl errors
  • Set up Google Analytics 4 and enquiry conversion events
  • Add relevant structured data where eligible
  • For local businesses, complete and maintain a Google Business Profile

SEO takes time and depends on competition, content quality, authority and technical health. These steps improve readiness and measurement; they do not guarantee a particular Google position or a fixed amount of traffic.

11 / FAQ

Questions clients
usually ask.

Can't find your answer? Message me on WhatsApp and I'll reply the same day.

How much does a website cost?

Starter websites begin at ₹19,990 and mobile apps at ₹49,000. Business websites and web applications are quoted on scope. Use the cost estimator above for a rough figure, then send me your requirements for an exact written quote.

How long does a project take?

A starter website usually takes 1–2 weeks. Business websites take 3–5 weeks, and apps or custom web applications take 6–12 weeks depending on features. You get a timeline with the proposal and a live preview link from the first week.

Will I be able to update the website myself?

Yes. Business websites and apps come with an admin panel where you can edit content, prices, offers and bookings without calling a developer. I also give you a short training session at handover.

Do you provide hosting, domain and maintenance?

I can set up hosting and your domain in your name, so you always own them. Hosting, domain and ongoing maintenance are quoted separately, and monthly maintenance plans are available after launch.

Can you guarantee a first-page Google ranking?

No honest developer can guarantee a ranking. What I do is build a fast, technically sound site with the right page structure, set up Search Console, Analytics and your Google Business Profile, and report on progress every month.

Do you work with clients outside India?

Yes. I have delivered projects for clients in the US, UK, Canada, Singapore, Dubai, Saudi Arabia, Kuwait and Bahrain. We work over WhatsApp, email and video calls, and I adjust meeting times to your time zone.

How do payments work?

Usually 40% to start, 40% after design and main build approval, and 20% at launch. Larger projects can be split into milestones. Bank transfer, UPI and international payments are accepted.
